**Sequencing logic (awareness → conversion).** Campaign 1, *"Marketing con IA para Escuelas — Conoce la Suite"*, runs first (Jul 1 – Aug 10) to build recognition of the Suite among private-education decision-makers before we ask for a meeting. It is weighted toward Meta for low-cost reach in MX and carries the reach KPI plus a modest lead floor. The two do not spend simultaneously, so the small budget is never split thin.

All client-facing lines are ES-MX, formal register, per the brand voice tokens.

**Trade-offs flagged.** At $600/quarter and a $22 CPL, paid spend alone yields roughly 27 leads; the 60-lead target leans on organic reach and diagnostic-call conversion. That gap is recorded as a coherence flag for the reviewer — it is not silently absorbed.
