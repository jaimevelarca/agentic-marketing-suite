LAUNCH BRIEF — Acme Co. BU · Campaign cmp-edu-convocatoria-ago (Convocatoria Agosto — Educacion Privada)

This run prepares a two-platform paid-media launch — nothing is live until the operator authorizes the $600/month spend. The campaign drives admissions-director leads for private schools in Mexico during the August enrollment window, pointing all paid traffic at the approved landing page lp-admisiones-agosto (/lp/guia-admisiones-agosto) and reusing the approved ad copy from 4.1.

Platform split and why: Meta gets $360/mo (60%) for broad lead-gen reach against the admissions-director interest profile — one well-funded ad set rather than several tiny ones, so the platform can actually exit its learning phase. Google Search gets $240/mo (40%) to capture high-intent demand: people already searching "como aumentar inscripciones escuela privada" or "marketing para colegios privados" convert at a lower CPL than cold reach. TikTok is deliberately omitted this cycle — on a small budget for a high-intent lead-gen goal, top-funnel TikTok awareness would dilute the spend; we revisit it at larger budgets.

Targeting logic: both ad sets serve the single campaign segment, seg-director-admisiones, geo-fenced to the metro markets where private-school decision-makers concentrate (CDMX, Estado de Mexico, Jalisco, Nuevo Leon), ages 30–60, ES-MX. Meta uses education-administration interests (no custom/lookalike audiences yet — the pre-launch BU has no seed list or pixel history); Google uses five high-intent ES-MX keywords with negative keywords ('gratis', 'empleo', 'becas') recommended to protect spend.

Creative and conversion wiring: each ad references an approved copy asset (cpy-acme-co-01 for Meta, -02 for the Google responsive search ad) and the lp-admisiones-agosto landing page. Visual creatives are not yet produced (4.2 pending), so visual_asset_ref is null and the launch is copy-driven for now. Conversion tracking optimizes the 'lead' / 'form_submit' events with per-platform UTM templates so 6.1 can attribute landing-page form submits back to each ad set.

The money: expected CPL is $22 (Meta) / $28 (Google) — SMB private-education benchmarks, not client history — implying roughly 23–24 leads/month at full spend, to be reconciled by 6.1 against the strategy's target CPL. Daily caps ($8 + $8) roll up under the $600 monthly ceiling; we recommend an account-level $600 spend cap as a hard guardrail. Two preconditions must be cleared before launch: the Meta Pixel and Google conversion tag must be installed on the landing page, and the operator must decide whether to launch copy-only or wait for visuals.
