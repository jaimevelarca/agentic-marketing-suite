## OUTPUT 2 — Creative Brief

CREATIVE BRIEF — Acme Co. BU · Cycle 2026-08

This batch realizes the visual-bearing items of the approved August content_plan: six specs across the two campaigns, with the two pure-text items (the week-3 WhatsApp broadcast and the conversion email) deliberately left without visuals.

Creative through-line: every asset shows a credible, warm, mid-market Mexican private-school world — admissions directors, small marketing teams, tasteful dashboards — never the sterile US-corporate stock look and never stereotype clichés. The brand system (teal #0F4C5C, orange #E36414, generous white space, clean sans-serif) carries across all six so the batch reads as one campaign.

Engine mix and why: Ideogram 3.0 handles the two text-led pieces (the week-1 funnel-leak carousel and the LinkedIn conversion creative) because legible Spanish typography is the whole point of those assets and Ideogram renders in-image text far more reliably than Flux. Flux 2 Pro handles the two photoreal stills (the Meta consideration creative and the landing-page hero) where the human/environment realism matters and text is added in post. Kling 2.5 produces the 8-second vertical awareness reel that opens Campaign A. Runway Gen-4.5 animates an approved workflow still into a 9-second demo for Campaign B, where controlled image-to-video motion beats text-to-video.

Funnel ladder: the reel opens awareness; the carousel, Meta creative, landing hero, and demo video carry consideration; the LinkedIn creative closes conversion. The landing-page hero (va-004) is the hinge — it must be rendered and live before the week-3 conversion push.

Brand styling: all six pin the two brand hexes; these are placeholders until the visual identity is packaged (S1-T7), so the reviewer should confirm the palette and supply the logo file before render.

fal.ai cost note: the two video assets (Kling reel, Runway demo) are the costliest lines in the batch. Both earn their place — one opens the high-intensity campaign, the other is the authority campaign's proof point — but the reviewer should confirm the spend before approving, since video is the second-largest variable cost line in production.
