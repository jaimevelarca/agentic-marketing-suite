## OUTPUT 2 — Build Brief

BUILD BRIEF — Acme Co. BU · Campaign cmp-edu-convocatoria-ago

This run produces one landing page, lp-admisiones-agosto, the conversion hinge of the August enrollment campaign. It serves a single objective — lead capture — for a single segment, the admissions director of a private school (seg-director-admisiones), and repeats exactly one primary CTA ("Descarga la guia de admisiones") in the hero and the final CTA. The page is consideration-stage: it offers a gated guide plus a diagnostic in exchange for a short, consented form, feeding the warm list that the week-3 WhatsApp/email conversion push (content_plan) will close.

Conversion logic: the hero leads with the segment's sharpest anxiety reframed as an outcome ("sin bajar la colegiatura"), reusing the approved hero copy asset. Honest authority replaces social proof because the client is pre-launch with no testimonials — we explicitly flag the gap rather than fabricate one. The problem and value-prop sections reuse approved copy_assets so the page message matches the rest of the funnel; benefits, how-it-works, FAQ, and all form microcopy are newly authored (marked source_copy_ref=null) because copy_assets did not cover them. The FAQ answers the segment's top objections — fit for small schools, time-to-value, no technical staff needed, data security — without making any pricing claim.

Form and consent: five fields, only one optional, with a required privacy-consent checkbox pointing to the aviso de privacidad (Mexico LFPDPPP expectation and the client's data_privacy constraint). A WhatsApp secondary CTA gives a lower-commitment path. Trust elements sit beside the form.

A/B plan: two single-element tests, each measurable with the page's analytics_events. ab-hero-headline tests pain-plus-outcome vs neutral-benefit framing on form_submit_rate. ab-form-length tests dropping the optional role field. We design the tests; Agent 6.3 declares winners once the data is in.

Accessibility & performance notes for the front-end (and Agent 4.2 for imagery): mobile-first layout, descriptive alt text on the hero image, form labels tied to inputs, sufficient contrast on the brand palette, a hero-image weight budget (<=150KB WebP) and an LCP target under 2.5s on 4G — LATAM traffic is mobile-dominant. The page is single-language ES-MX throughout; no internal cost/margin figures appear anywhere.
