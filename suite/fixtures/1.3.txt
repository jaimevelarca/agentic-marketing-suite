=== COMPETITIVE BRIEF — Acme Co. ===
Markets in scope: Mexico · Colombia · Dominican Republic · Puerto Rico
Competitive set: 4 competitors (0 client-named, 1 discovered, 3 AI-inferred)

THE FIELD
The real fight for an SMB marketing budget in LATAM is not against a single named rival — it is against four archetypes: the premium local agency (human, expensive, no ROI transparency), the self-serve software platform (HubSpot — capable but leaves the SMB to execute), the cheap marketplace freelancer, and the status-quo in-house hire / do-it-yourself. Acme Co. competes with all four at once because it offers the agency's outcome at the software's price.

WHERE WE WIN / WHERE WE'RE EXPOSED
- Win: agency-grade, done-for-you output at SMB subscription prices, with ROI measured every month — a combination none of the four can match.
- Exposed: pre-launch with no named-client proof yet; brand unknown against HubSpot and established agencies; conservative SMB owners may distrust AI-run marketing.

WHITE SPACE
Done-for-you outcomes with transparent monthly ROI, delivered Spanish-first to LATAM SMBs at a price agencies cannot reach.

TOP ANGLES TO ACT ON (for Layers 3-4)
1. "ROI por peso, medible cada mes" — lead with transparent measured results no competitor shows. (high · LinkedIn, Instagram, email)
