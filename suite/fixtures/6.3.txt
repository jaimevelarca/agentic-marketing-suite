## OUTPUT 2 — Optimization Brief

OPTIMIZATION BRIEF — Acme Co. BU · Cycle 2026-08

Performance this cycle was a clear story of one channel and one creative angle carrying the account. The Instagram carousel A/B test reached a decisive result: the variant that leads with a concrete cost-per-enrolled-lead proof point booked 41 leads at $13.90 CPL versus 22 leads at $26.40 for the generic brand hook — roughly 2x more efficient, on a sample well past the decision floor, and beating the $18 CPL contract. That test is concluded and the proof-point hook is now the default for carousels; the lesson is written back to Layer 4 (copy) and Layer 3 (planning).

Four of the five actions this cycle are bounded and have been auto-applied: the carousel A/B conclusion, pausing the LinkedIn authority article (CPL ~$61 against a $30 target, the same decay two cycles running), refreshing the fatigued Meta creative (CTR halved from 1.9% to 0.8% as frequency crossed 4 while CPL held — a creative problem, not an audience one), and holding the WhatsApp send-time test, which is still too thin (41 opens / 6 conversions) to call without overfitting.

The one item that needs your sign-off is a budget move. LinkedIn is the worst channel against target ($58 blended CPL) and Meta is the best ($16); the highest-leverage shift is to take the freed LinkedIn-article spend and concentrate it on the winning Meta carousel campaign. That move is $200, which is 33% of the LinkedIn channel budget — above the 20% line that separates a bounded reallocation from a binding spend decision — so it is proposed, not applied. It is a pure shift (total spend unchanged, within the client's ceiling), and approving it should pull blended CPL down a further 10-15% next cycle. Partner sign-off per the approval chain is the only thing holding it.

Net expected effect if the reallocation is approved: blended CPL down meaningfully against the August baseline, with the proof-point hook and the refreshed Meta creative compounding the gain. The durable lessons going back to L3/L4 are: lead carousels with a cost-per-lead proof point; keep LinkedIn for organic authority, not paid lead-gen; and pre-schedule a Meta creative refresh every ~2-3 weeks.
