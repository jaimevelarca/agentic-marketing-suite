## OUTPUT 2 — Production Brief

PRODUCTION BRIEF — Acme Co. BU · Cycle 2026-08

This batch turns the August editorial plan into four ready-to-review assets plus their image-gen prompts. The funnel mix is 1 awareness / 2 consideration / 1 conversion, deliberately consideration-weighted because the pilot is still building its first warm list before pushing conversion spend.

Copy and segments. The awareness reel (asset 01) and the Meta creative (asset 03) speak to the admissions-director segment on the "ROI medible" pillar — one soft (seasonal pain, no numbers), one sharper (cost-per-enrolled-lead framing). The consideration carousel (asset 02) and the WhatsApp broadcast (asset 04) speak to the school-marketing segment on "Operacion sin friccion" — the carousel surfaces the funnel-leak problem and drives the lead magnet; the broadcast converts that captured list ahead of the deadline. Every hook leads with the buyer's pain or payoff, never the brand name, and leans into the competitive white-space (measurement + frictionless operation) rather than feature lists.

A/B variants. Assets 01, 02 and 03 ship with alternative hooks so Layer 6 can test: process-vs-luck and listicle openings on the reel, a volume-vs-leakage reframe on the carousel, and a CPL-vs-clicks metric contrast on the ad. The WhatsApp broadcast ships without variants (single personalized send).

Image prompts for Agent 4.2. Three prompts total. Two go to flux-2-pro (photoreal hero shots — the admissions-director reel cover and the dashboard ad creative — both with no in-image text and negative prompts that forbid fabricated on-screen numbers and competitor logos). One goes to ideogram-3.0: the carousel cover, which needs the legible Spanish line "3 fugas en tu embudo" rendered in-image. The WhatsApp broadcast is text-only, so it carries no image prompt. All prompts carry an aspect ratio matched to the channel (9:16 reel, 1:1 feed/ad) and ES-MX alt text for accessibility.

Constraint calls. No prices, discounts, or colegiatura cuts anywhere (pricing-claims). No internal cost/margin figures (confidentiality). No specific performance numbers were invented — the ROI creative frames the measurement angle without quoting a figure that isn't in the data. The WhatsApp broadcast is flagged for opt-in/data-privacy confirmation.

Assets needing human review: 02 (medium — lead-magnet CTA + process claim), 03 (high — ROI/measurement framing on paid), 04 (medium — outbound WhatsApp under opt-in/data-privacy). Asset 01 (awareness reel) is low-risk and could be spot-checked.
